Kota Bunny Chow
With Chips

This is kind of like a bunny chow, faster food.
Instead, the quarter loaf of bread is filled with french fries,
ham, chakalaka, eggs and chips.In the quiet town of Kota
Chow, nestled between rolling green hills and whispering
bamboo groves, lived a curious little bunny named Momo.
Unlike the other rabbits, Momo wore a tiny red scarf and
had a nose for adventure. Every morning, she would hop
through the cobbled streets of Kota Chow, visiting the tea shops and greeting the friendly shopkeepers. Children loved to follow her as she explored, always hoping she'd lead them to one of her secret hideouts — especially the one near the old pagoda, where wildflowers grew in rainbow clusters and the breeze always carried the sweet scent of jasmine.
